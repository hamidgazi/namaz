## 🌟 Introduction & Philosophy

Prayer (*Salah*) in Islam is strictly bound to specific astronomical times ordained by Allah ﷻ:
> **إِنَّ الصَّلَاةَ كَانَتْ عَلَى الْمُؤْمِنِينَ كِتَابًا مَّوْقُوتًا**  
> *"Indeed, prayer has been decreed upon the believers at prescribed times."* (Surah An-Nisa: 103)

In the definitive Hanafi legal encyclopedia **Bahar-e-Shariat** (*Volume 1, Part 3: Auqat-e-Namaz*), **Sadrush Shariah Allama Qadi Amjad Ali Azmi** compiles the exact limits, recommended (*Mustahabb*) intervals, and disliked (*Makruh*) periods for each prayer based on the *Ahadith*, *al-Hidayah*, *Radd al-Muhtar*, and the astronomical observations of Imam Ahmad Raza Khan (*Fatawa Razawiyya*).

This application translates these sacred rulings into an intuitive, ambient desktop widget and web app that uses **proven visual cognitive psychology** to send clear, stress-free signals to the brain about prayer urgency.

## 🎨 The Psychological Color Engine: Why These Colors?

Human visual cognition perceives color before conscious text processing. On computer screens and ambient displays, color temperature and luminance directly trigger neurological responses:

$$\begin{array}{ccl}
\text{\Large 🟢} & \textbf{Emerald Green (\#10B981)} & \textbf{Psychological State: Peace, Tranquility \& Khushu' (Itmi'nan)} \\
\text{\Large 🟡} & \textbf{Solar Gold (\#FBBF24)} & \textbf{Psychological State: Gentle Focus \& Awareness (Intibah)} \\
\text{\Large 🟠} & \textbf{Sunset Orange (\#F97316)} & \textbf{Psychological State: Urgent Action \& Wrap-Up (Inzar)} \\
\text{\Large 🔴} & \textbf{Crimson Red (\#EF4444)} & \textbf{Psychological State: Critical Alert \& Immediate Obligation (Khatar)}
\end{array}$$

### 1. 🟢 Emerald Green (`#10B981` / `#34D399`) — *Safe & Virtuous Window*
* **Neurological Impact**: The human eye is most sensitive to green wavelengths (~555 nm). Green reduces cortisol levels, relaxes the nervous system, and provides a sense of abundant time and security.
* **Islamic Significance**: Green is the beloved color of Islam, the dome of the Prophet's Mosque ﷺ, and the garments of Paradise (*Surah Al-Insan: 21*).
* **When Shown**: During the safe, comfortable major portion of each prayer window (e.g. $>25$ min remaining in Maghrib, $>45$ min in Zuhr, $>35$ min in Asr). It tells the brain: *"You have ample time. Pray with full concentration and peace of mind."*

### 2. 🟡 Solar Gold (`#FBBF24`) — *Transition & Recommended Window*
* **Neurological Impact**: High luminance without the aggressiveness of red. Stimulates mental alertness and active recall without inducing panic or stress.
* **Islamic Significance**: Represents the warmth of the sun and the transition into *Waqt-e-Mustahabb* (such as broad daylight *Isfar* in Fajr, or the midway milestone in Zuhr and Asr).
* **When Shown**: When a prayer enters its second half or approaches its final third. It tells the brain: *"Time is advancing gracefully. Finish your current task and prepare for prayer."*

### 3. 🟠 Sunset Orange (`#F97316`) — *Urgent Warning & Makruh Horizon*
* **Neurological Impact**: Warm, high-urgency color that commands attention. Triggers dopamine-driven motivation to wrap up pending activities immediately.
* **Islamic Significance**: In nature, this is the literal color of *Isfirar ash-Shams* (when the setting sun turns pale orange/yellow before sunset). In Fiqh, this marks the entrance into *Makruh* windows:
  * **Asr**: The final 20 minutes before Maghrib (*Makruh Tahrimi*).
  * **Maghrib**: The final 15 minutes as stars cluster densely (*Makruh Tahrimi*).
  * **Zuhr**: The final 25 minutes (where $>90\%$ of Zuhr has expired).
* **When Shown**: In the final warning stretches of each prayer. It tells the brain: *"Do not delay further. Stop working and go pray now."*

### 4. 🔴 Crimson Red Alert (`#EF4444`) + Breathing Pulse — *Critical Emergency / Prohibition*
* **Neurological Impact**: Triggers fight-or-flight awareness, elevating visual priority above all other software windows on your screen. The breathing pulse and live ticking seconds prevent complacency.
* **Islamic Significance**: Represents the boundaries of prohibition (*Awqat-e-Mamnoo'ah*) and the imminent danger of letting an obligatory prayer become *Qadha* (missed).
* **When Shown**:
  * During **Strictly Prohibited Times**: Midday Zenith (*Zawal* ~10 min before Zuhr) and Sunrise (*Tulu* for 20 minutes).
  * During **Critical Expiry**: Final 8–12 minutes of any prayer.

## 📜 Complete Prayer Rulings from *Bahar-e-Shariat*

### 1. 🌅 FAJR (صبح صادق تا طلوعِ آفتاب)
* **Astronomical Boundaries** (*Bahar-e-Shariat, Vol 1, Page 451*):
  * **Begins**: *Subh Sadiq* (True Dawn) — when a white light spreads horizontally along the eastern horizon.
  * **Ends**: *Tulu-e-Aftab* (Sunrise) — the moment the upper rim of the sun's disc appears on the horizon.
* **Recommended (*Mustahabb*) Times** (*Page 455*):
  * **For Men**: *Isfar* (إسفار) — delaying until dawn has brightened and the ground is clearly illuminated. However, one must start early enough that 40 to 60 verses can be recited with calm rhythm (*Tartil*), and if an error occurs, wudhu can be repeated and the prayer performed again before sunrise.
  * **For Women** (*Page 456, Masla 7*): Always *Ghalas* (غلس) — praying at the earliest dawn while darkness remains.
* **Disliked (*Makruh*) Times**:
  * Delaying so late that there is doubt whether the sun has already risen is *Makruh*.
  * If the sun's rim breaches the horizon while praying Fajr, the prayer is invalidated and must be repeated as *Qadha*.
* **Our App's Color Logic**:
  * **$> 30$ min left**: 🟢 **Emerald Green** (Calm safe window).
  * **$18 - 30$ min left**: 🟡 **Solar Gold** (*Isfar* broad daylight window).
  * **$10 - 18$ min left**: 🟠 **Sunset Orange** (Sunrise approaching rapidly).
  * **$< 10$ min left**: 🔴 **Crimson Red Alert** (Imminent sunrise danger with live seconds).

---

### 2. ☀️ MORNING & ISHRAQ / CHASHT (طلوع تا زوال)
* **Sunrise Prohibition (*Tulu*)** (*Page 456 Masla 12 & Page 458 Masla 21*):
  > *"تجربہ سے ثابت ہوا کہ قرصِ آفتاب میں یہ زردی اس وقت آ جاتی ہے جب غروب میں بیس منٹ باقی رہتے ہیں... یوں ہی بعدِ طلوع بیس منٹ کے بعد جوازِ نماز کا وقت ہو جاتا ہے۔"*
  * For exactly **20 minutes after sunrise**, all prayers (Fard, Qadha, Nafl, Sajdah Tilawat) are **strictly prohibited** (*Haram / Makruh Tahrimi*).
* **Ishraq Window (اشراق)**:
  * Begins at **Sunrise + 20 minutes** (when the sun rises 1 spear length and eyes are dazzled).
  * Lasts until approximately 45–50 minutes after sunrise.
* **Chasht / Salat al-Duha (چاشت)**:
  * Mid-morning voluntary prayer. Most virtuous when one-fourth of the day has elapsed.
* **Zawal Zenith (*Nisf-un-Nahar*)** (*Page 458 Masla 21*):
  * The **10 to 12 minutes immediately preceding Dhuhr** is Midday Zenith (*Istiwa*). All prayers are strictly forbidden.
* **Our App's Logic**:
  * Displays **`NEXT: DHUHR`** (or **`NEXT: JUMMAH`** on Friday).
  * **0 to 20 min**: 🚫 `SUNRISE PROHIBITION` shield (`Wait 20m for Ishraq`).
  * **20 to 50 min**: Subtext shows `Ishraq Sunnah active • Dhuhr at {Time}`.
  * **50 min to Zawal**: Subtext shows `Chasht (Duha) time • Dhuhr at {Time}`.
  * **Last 10 min**: 🚫 `ZAWAL PROHIBITION` shield + **🔴 Crimson Red Alert** (`Zawal Zenith • All prayers prohibited`).

---

### 3. 🌤️ DHUHR & JUMMAH (زوال تا مثلِ ثانی)
* **Astronomical Boundaries** (*Page 453*):
  * **Begins**: Immediately when the sun crosses the meridian (*Zawal* / declination).
  * **Ends**: *Misl-e-Sani* (Hanafi 2x shadow factor) — when an object's shadow becomes twice its length plus its noon shadow (*Zill-e-Asli*).
* **Mustahabb Times** (*Page 456, Masla 8*):
  * **Summer**: *Ibrad* (إبراد) — delaying until the intense midday heat cools down.
  * **Winter**: *Ta'jeel* (early prayer) is Mustahabb.
  * **Jummah**: Follows the same Mustahabb timing as Dhuhr.
* **Permissibility Across the Whole Window** (*Page 461, Masla 35*):
  > *"فجر اور ظہر کے پورے وقت اول سے آخر تک بلا کراہت ہیں ۔ یعنی یہ نمازیں اپنے وقت کے جس حصے میں پڑھی جائیں اصلاً مکروہ نہیں ۔"*
  * Unlike Asr or Maghrib, Dhuhr has no inherently Makruh end period. However, delaying until Asr arrives causes the prayer to be missed.
* **Our App's Color Logic**:
  * **$> 45$ min left**: 🟢 **Emerald Green** (Long comfortable window).
  * **$25 - 45$ min left**: 🟡 **Solar Gold** (Wrap-up heads up).
  * **$12 - 25$ min left**: 🟠 **Sunset Orange** ($>90\%$ of Dhuhr has elapsed).
  * **$< 12$ min left**: 🔴 **Crimson Red Alert** (Final minutes before Asr).

---

### 4. 🌇 ASR (مثلِ ثانی تا غروبِ آفتاب)
* **Astronomical Boundaries** (*Page 454*):
  * **Begins**: When an object's shadow reaches 2x its length plus noon shadow.
  * **Ends**: Complete sunset (*Ghurub-e-Aftab*).
* **Mustahabb Times** (*Page 456, Masla 10 & 13*):
  * Delaying Asr into the second half of its time is Mustahabb for men to allow congregation to assemble.
* **The 20-Minute Makruh Tahrimi Window (*Isfirar ash-Shams*)** (*Page 456 Masla 12 & Page 458 Masla 21*):
  > *"البتہ اس روز اگر عصر کی نماز نہیں پڑھی تو اگرچہ آفتاب ڈوبتا ہو پڑھ لے مگر اتنی تاخیر کرنا حرام ہے ۔ حدیث میں اس کو منافق کی نماز فرمایا... اور اس وقت سے کہ آفتاب پر نگاہ ٹھہرنے لگے ڈوبنے تک غروب ہے یہ وقت بھی ۲۰ منٹ ہے"*
  * When **20 minutes remain before Maghrib**, the sun's disc pales and loses radiance. Delaying Fard until this time without an excuse is a major sin (*Haram*). All voluntary prayers are forbidden. Only today's delayed Fard prayer may be performed.
* **Our App's Color Logic**:
  * **$> 35$ min left**: 🟢 **Emerald Green** (Safe early window).
  * **$20 - 35$ min left**: 🟡 **Solar Gold** (Virtuous second-half delay).
  * **$\le 20$ min left**: 🟠 **Sunset Orange** + Subtitle: `Iftar at {Time} • ⚠️ Makruh Time`.
  * **$< 10$ min left**: 🔴 **Crimson Red Alert** (Sunset imminent).

---

### 5. 🌆 MAGHRIB (غروب تا غروبِ شفقِ ابیض)
* **Astronomical Boundaries** (*Page 454 & 455*):
  * **Begins**: The exact moment the sun's disc disappears beneath the western horizon.
  * **Ends**: *Ghurub-e-Shafaq-e-Abyad* — when the white twilight spreading along the horizon disappears completely (Karachi 18° twilight / Isha start).
* **Mustahabb Times** (*Page 457, Masla 15*):
  > *"روزِ ابر کے سوا مغرب میں ہمیشہ تعجیل مستحب ہے اور دو رکعت سے زائد کی تاخیر مکروہِ تنزیہی اور اگر بغیر عذرِ سفر و مرض وغیرہ اتنی تاخیر کی کہ ستارے گتھ گئے تو مکروہِ تحریمی۔"*
  * **Ta'jeel (Prompt prayer)** is universally Mustahabb.
  * Delaying past the time of 2 short rakats (~8–10 min) is *Makruh Tanzihi*.
  * Delaying without an excuse until stars cluster densely (*Sitara-e-Guth gaye*, final 15–20 minutes) is **Makruh Tahrimi**!
* **Why 44 Minutes is 100% Green**:
  * In Surat, Maghrib lasts ~75 minutes. At 44 minutes remaining, more than 60% of the window remains. Twilight is bright. It is completely valid and calm.
* **Our App's Color Logic**:
  * **$> 25$ min left**: 🟢 **Emerald Green** (Peaceful, ample time; 44m is 100% green).
  * **$15 - 25$ min left**: 🟡 **Solar Gold** (Dusk deepening).
  * **$\le 15$ min left**: 🟠 **Sunset Orange** + Subtitle: `Ends at {Time} • ⚠️ Makruh Time (Stars clustered)`.
  * **$< 8$ min left**: 🔴 **Crimson Red Alert** (Imminent Isha adhan).

---

### 6. 🌌 ISHA & WITR (غروبِ شفقِ ابیض تا طلوعِ فجر)
* **Astronomical Boundaries** (*Page 455*):
  * **Begins**: When the white twilight vanishes.
  * **Ends**: True Dawn (*Subh Sadiq* / Fajr). Total duration: ~9 to 10 hours.
* **Mustahabb, Mubah & Makruh Windows** (*Page 457, Masla 16*):
  > *"عشا میں تہائی رات تک تاخیر مستحب ہے اور آدھی رات تک تاخیر مباح یعنی جب کہ آدھی رات ہونے سے پہلے فرض پڑھ چکے اور اتنی تاخیر کہ رات ڈھل گئی مکروہ ہے کہ باعثِ تقلیلِ جماعت ہے۔"*
  * **First 1/3 of the Night (*Thuluth-e-Layl*)**: **Mustahabb** (Recommended).
  * **Up to Islamic Midnight (*Nisf-ul-Layl*)**: **Mubah** (Permissible).
  * **Past Islamic Midnight**: **Makruh Tanzihi** without a valid necessity.
* **Witr & Tahajjud** (*Page 457, Masla 18*):
  * If one is confident in waking before dawn, delaying Witr to the last third of the night with Tahajjud is Mustahabb.
* **Our App's Color Logic**:
  * **Thuluth to Nisf-ul-Layl**: 🟢 **Emerald Green**.
  * **Past Midnight**: Subtitle alerts: `Past Midnight (Makruh delay) • Sehri ends at {Fajr}`.
  * **$\le 30$ min before Fajr**: 🟠 **Sunset Orange** (Dawn approaching).
  * **$< 15$ min before Fajr**: 🔴 **Crimson Red Alert** (Final moments before Fajr adhan).

## 🚫 The 3 Strictly Prohibited Times (*Awqat-e-Mamnoo'ah*)
*Bahar-e-Shariat, Vol 1, Page 458, Masla 21*

In these 3 specific times, **NO PRAYER WHATSOEVER** is permissible (neither Fard, Qadha, Nafl, nor Janazah, nor Sajdah Tilawat):
1. **Sunrise (*Tulu*)**: From sunrise for **20 minutes** until the sun rises high and eyes are dazzled.
2. **Midday Zenith (*Zawal / Istiwa*)**: The **10 to 12 minutes** immediately preceding Dhuhr.
3. **Sunset (*Isfirar ash-Shams*)**: The final **20 minutes** of Asr until the sun completely disappears (only today's delayed Asr Fard is allowed if unperformed).
