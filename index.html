Settings & Methodology

Calibrated for Indian Sunni Masajid

Jurisprudence (Fiqh) Hanafi Default

Asr is strictly calculated at Misl-e-Sani (shadow = 2× object height + noon shadow) per Imam Abu Hanifa (رحمه الله).

Astronomical Convention Karachi 18°/18°

Fajr at 18.0° depression; Isha at 18.0° Shafaq Abyad (white twilight disappearance). Consensus across Indian Masajid.

Precautionary Margins (Ihtiyat)
Clock Rounding Direction RoundForward

Rounds up to next whole minute for prayer bounds so Salah is never performed early. Floors seconds for Sehri cut-off.
